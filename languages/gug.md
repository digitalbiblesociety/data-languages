Paraguayan Guarani, or simply Guarani, is a language of South America that belongs to the Tupi–Guarani branch of the Tupian language family. It is one of the two official languages of Paraguay, where it is spoken by the majority of the population, and where half of the rural population are monolingual speakers of the language.
