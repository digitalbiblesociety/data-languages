Ezaa is an Igboid language spoken in Ebonyi state in Nigeria. It forms a dialect cluster with closely related Izii, Ikwo, and Mgbo, though they are only marginally mutually intelligible.
Ezaa language consists 2 tonal patterns of speakers the broader Ezza language and the ukawu tone only a different of word patterns in pronunciation and intonation.
The phonetic alphabet of the Ezza language is 40 in number due to huge tonal features present in it compared to the standardized Igbo with 36.
