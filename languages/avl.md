Northwest Arabian Arabic is a proposed subfamily of Arabic encompassing the traditional Bedouin dialects of the Sinai Peninsula and Egyptian Eastern Desert, the Negev, Gaza Strip, southern Jordan, and the northwestern corner of Saudi Arabia.
