Kamayurá is an Indigenous language of Brazil and a member of the Tupi–Guarani family, spoken by the Kamayurá people of Brazil, who numbered about 600 individuals in 2014. The Kamayurá people live in the Mato Grosso region of Brazil, specifically in the Upper Xingu area.
