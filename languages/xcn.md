Cotoname is an Indigenous language of Mexico and the American state of Texas formerly spoken by Native Americans indigenous to the lower Rio Grande Valley of northeastern Mexico and extreme southern Texas. Today it is extinct, and is proposed to be a component of a Pakawan language family. Today, it is considered a language isolate.
