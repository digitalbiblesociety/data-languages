Sirmauri is a Western Pahari language spoken in the Sirmaur district in the northern Indian state of Himachal Pradesh and in Kalesar area of Yamunanagar district of Haryana and in border areas of Uttarakhand. Its two main varieties are Dharthi and Giripari.
