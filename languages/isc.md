Isconahua or Iscobaquebo is an indigenous American language of the Panoan family. It is spoken by the Isconahua tribe in Peru. The Isconahua is a very isolated tribe and has very little contact with the outside world. In spite of this, as of 2023 the language is spoken by only one person, Nawa Nika, when language preservation efforts were underway.
