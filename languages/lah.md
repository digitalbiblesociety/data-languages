Lahnda, also known as Western Punjabi, is a proposed grouping of northwestern Indo-Aryan language varieties spoken primarily in Punjab, and most of Azad Kashmir in Pakistan. The term was introduced into linguistic literature by George Abraham Grierson during the compilation of the Linguistic Survey of India in the early 20th century.
