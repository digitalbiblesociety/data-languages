Kanjari is an Indo-Aryan language associated with the Kanjar people of India and Pakistan. Kanjari is spoken in Punjab and parts of Balochistan in Pakistan, and Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan in India. UNESCO classifies Kanjari as an endangered language.
