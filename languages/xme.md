Median is an extinct Iranian language which was spoken by the ancient Medes. It belongs to the Northwestern branch of the Iranian language family, which includes many other much more recently attested and different languages such as the Kurdish languages, Tati dialects, Talysh, the central Iranian dialects, the Caspian dialects Gilaki, Mazanderani and Zaza.
