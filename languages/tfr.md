Teribe is a Chibchan language of the Talamanca subgroup spoken by the Naso or Teribe people. It is spoken primarily in the Naso Tjër Di Comarca and the Bocas del Toro Province of northwestern Panama and in the southern part of Costa Rica's Puntarenas Province, but is almost extinct in the latter. It is part of the Chibchan language family. There were about 3,000 speakers as of 2000, nearly all of whom speak Spanish as well.
