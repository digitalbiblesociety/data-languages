The Ambai or Ampari is an Austronesian language spoken by the Ambai people in Indonesian New Guinea, mostly on the Ambai Islands, as well as the southern part of Yapen Island. The number of speakers is estimated to be 10,000. Dialects are Randawaya, Ambai (Wadapi-Laut), and Manawi.
