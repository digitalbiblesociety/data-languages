San Miguel Creole is a dialect of Lesser Antillean French Creole spoken by the descendants of St Lucians and Martiniquais who emigrated to Panama in the 19th century. They came over as construction workers on the Panama Canal. There is very little information on the language and therefore it is likely an extinct language in Panama. There probably are remnants in local elder communities of the language, probably words and phrases, however the language is considered lost and extinct by most, with no sources on the vocabulary.
