Ghɔmáláʼ or Ghomala is a major Bamileke language spoken in Cameroon, originally in the following departments of the West region:Mifi, Koung-Khi and Hauts-Plateaux: most of the three departments 
Menoua: east of the department
Bamboutos: a corner in the south
