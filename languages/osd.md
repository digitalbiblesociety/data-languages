Digor Ossetian, also known as Digor Ossetic or Digor-Ossetic, is a dialect of the Ossetian language spoken by the Digor subgroup. It is far less widely spoken than the Iron dialect, the standard Ossetian dialect. The two are distinct enough to sometimes be considered separate languages; in his 2003 Digor–Russian dictionary, the compiler Fedar Takazov refers to a "Digor language", but the editor in the same book uses "Digor dialect".
