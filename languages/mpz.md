Mpi is a Loloish language of Thailand. The number of speakers is in decline. It is spoken in the following two villages in northern Thailand.Ban Dong (บ้านดง), Tambon Suan Khuean (สวนเขื่อน), Mueang Phrae District, Phrae Province 
Ban Sakoen (บ้านสะเกิน), Tambon Yot (ยอด), Song Khwae District, Nan Province
