Liangmai language or Lianglad is a Sino-Tibetan language spoken by the Liangmai Naga community in the Naga Hills in the northeast part of India, in the states of Nagaland and Manipur. It has been called Kwoireng and is particularly close to Zeme, Inpui and Rongmei. Legend has it that Lianglad is the language of the gods or charah lad in Lianglad. The pagans from the Rongmei tribe are known to still be using lianglad while performing rituals. Before the advent of Christianity, the people practiced paganism. Thus, according to their belief, nature, like mountains, caves, rivers, trees, etc., was ruled or governed by gods, and it is these gods that speak Lianglad.
