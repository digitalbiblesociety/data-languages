Seberuang is an Ibanic language spoken in the province of West Kalimantan, Indonesia. It is spoken by the Seberuang Dayak people who mainly live in Sintang Regency and parts of Sekadau Regency. In addition, the speakers also live along the upper Seberuang River and also on the southern provincial road that connects Semitau with Putussibau, the capital of Kapuas Hulu Regency.
