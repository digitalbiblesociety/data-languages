Nding is an endangered Niger–Congo language in the Talodi family of Kordofan, Sudan. Nding is spoken in the area of the mountain (Jebel) Eliri, on the south of Talodi in South Kordofan, Sudan. Because of that, the language also goes by the name Eliri.
