Ayoquesco Zapotec is a small Zapotec language of Oaxaca, Mexico.
Ayoquesco Zapotec (Dísè) is spoken in Santa María Ayoquezco and Santa Cruz Nexila, around 50% intelligible to San Andres Zabache speakers.
