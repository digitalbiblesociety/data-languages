Hui Chinese is one of the ten primary varieties of the Chinese language spoken in the Hui cultural region, including the historical Huizhou Prefecture in Southern Anhui, and part of the historical Yanzhou Prefecture in Zhejiang and part of Raozhou Prefecture in Jiangxi.
