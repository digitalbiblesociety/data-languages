Iranian Sign Language (ISL), also referred to as Farsi Sign Language, Persian Sign Language, and Zaban Eshareh Irani, is the sign language used by Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in Iran. It is a true sign language, unlike the Baghcheban phonetic hand alphabet, which is a form of cued speech.
