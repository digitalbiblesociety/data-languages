Kusunda or Kusanda is a language isolate spoken by a few among the Kusunda people in western and central Nepal. As of 2023, it only has a single fluent speaker, Kamala Sen-Khatri, although there are efforts underway to keep the language alive. There were 23 native speakers according to the 2021 Nepal census.
