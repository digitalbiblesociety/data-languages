El Hugeirat is a moribund Hill Nubian language spoken in the northern Nuba Mountains of southern Sudan. It is spoken by approximately 50 people in a few families within the El Hugeirat hills, specifically in the villages of Sija, Bija, Shenishen, and Baboy.
