Mlaḥsô or Mlahso, sometimes referred to as Suryoyo or Surayt, is an extinct or dormant Central Neo-Aramaic language. It was traditionally spoken in eastern Turkey and later also in northeastern Syria by ethnically Assyrian Syriac Orthodox Christians.
