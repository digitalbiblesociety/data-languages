Galatian is an extinct Celtic language once spoken by the Galatians in Galatia, in central Anatolia, from the 3rd century BC up to at least the 4th century AD. Some sources suggest that it was still spoken in the 6th century. Galatian was contemporary with, and closely related to, Gaulish. It has been described as a variant or dialect of the Gaulish language.
