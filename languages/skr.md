Saraiki is an Indo-Aryan language spoken mainly in southern Punjab, and also in parts of northern Sindh and southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in Pakistan. According to the 2023 Pakistani census, Saraiki is spoken by 28.84 million people, making it one of the most widely spoken languages in Pakistan.
