Marimanindji (Marramanindjdji) is an extinct Australian Aboriginal language of the Daly River region in the Northern Territory. No fluent speakers remained by 2016. The name means 'what language?' and is derived from mara- 'language' and manandjil 'what'. The primary description of Marimanindji is a sketch grammar by Tryon (1974).
