Konjo is a Bantu language spoken primarily by the Konjo people in Uganda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The language belongs to the Northeast Bantu subgroup and demonstrates significant linguistic similarities with neighboring languages in the Great Lakes region of Africa. It has a 77% lexical similarity with Nande. There are many dialects, including Sanza (Ekisanza).
