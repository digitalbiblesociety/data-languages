Fijian is an Austronesian language of the Malayo-Polynesian family spoken by some 350,000–450,000 ethnic Fijians as a native language. In the 2013 constitution, Fijian is a national language of Fiji, along with English, Standard Hindi, and Fiji Hindi. Fijian is a VOS language.
