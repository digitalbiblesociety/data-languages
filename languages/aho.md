Ahom or Tai Ahom is a Southwestern Tai language spoken as a second languageby some Ahom people. It is currently undergoing a revival and mainly used in religious and educational purposes. Ahom language was the state language of Ahom kingdom. It was relatively free of both Mon-Khmer and Indo-Aryan influences and has a written tradition dating back to the 13th century.
