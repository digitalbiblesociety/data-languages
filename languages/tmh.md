The Tuareg languages are a branch of Berber varieties spoken by the Tuareg people in regions of Mali, Niger, Algeria, Libya and Burkina Faso, with a few speakers, the Kinnin, in Chad. The four Tuareg languages are Tamahaq, Tamasheq, Air Tamajeq and Tawellemmet.
