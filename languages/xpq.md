Mohegan-Pequot is an Algonquian language formerly spoken, and currently being reclaimed, by the Indigenous peoples of southern present-day New England and eastern Long Island. Dialects in New England included Mohegan, Pequot, and Niantic; and on Long Island, Montaukett and Shinnecock.
