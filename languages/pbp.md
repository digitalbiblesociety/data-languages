Badyara is an Eastern Senegal-Guinea language of Guinea and Guinea-Bissau. Variants of the name are Badara, Badian, Badjara, Badyaranké; there are also Jaad, Bigola, Gola, Kanjad [ka-njad(ɛ)]), Pajade, Pajadinka.
