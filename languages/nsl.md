Norwegian Sign Language, or NSL, is the principal sign language in Norway. There are many sign language organizations and some television programs broadcast in NSL in Norway. The Norwegian Broadcasting Corporation airs Nyheter på tegnspråk daily and previously aired Tid for tegn through the years 2008-2011.
