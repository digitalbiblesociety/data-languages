Twi-Fante, commonly know by the broader term Akan is the most widely spoken language of Ghana, and the main native language of the Akan people, spoken over much of the southern half of Ghana. It is generally referred to by its speakers as either Twi or Fante. 
About 44% of Ghanaians are native speakers, and about 80% of Ghana's population speak Akan as a first or second language, while the Bono dialect is also spoken across the border in Ivory Coast.
